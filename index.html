History on Gymnastics

Gymnastic's history dates back thousands of years. Ancient Greeks used gymnastics as a form of physcial training for their soldiers, while the Romans used it for entertainment. It wasn't until the 1800's that gymnastics began to modernize into what we know it as today. Germany developed parallel bars and the pommel horse for example, two events we see still today in men's gymnastics.
